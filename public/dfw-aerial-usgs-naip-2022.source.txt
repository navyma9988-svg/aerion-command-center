Alignment contract
  These bounds are the inverse projection of src/lib/airfield-geo.ts at world
  coordinates (0,0) and (1200,762). They include the existing 64-world-unit
  perimeter padding. Place the image at SVG x=0, y=0, width=1200, height=762,
  with preserveAspectRatio="none". Do not alter runway coordinates, WORLD,
  SCALE, padding, or the radar viewBox.

Alignment caveats
  The application uses a local equirectangular projection centred at
  32.894 N, 97.041 W. The EPSG:4326 export is intentionally stretched into
  the same local aspect ratio; this is required for the existing surveyed
  vector geometry to coincide with the image.
  Source imagery is a mosaic acquired on two dates, so subtle colour seams or
  aircraft/vehicle position differences are expected. It is context imagery,
  not current operational surveillance.
  Use a dark navy colour wash and reduced opacity in the UI so operational
  vectors, labels, focus rings, and status colours retain WCAG contrast.
